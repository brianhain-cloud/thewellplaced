HOW TO ADD THEM
  1. Drop images into photos-raw/ named after the entry, any
     reasonable spelling. The filename below is the safest bet.
  2. python3 process_photos.py
  3. python3 build/splice.py is not needed; the script writes
     straight into public/index.html. Then npx wrangler deploy.

  Anything the script cannot match is listed when it finishes, so
  a near miss on the filename is recoverable.
